## Before you start


Does “free” mean I can download a PDF here?

No. An external free resource may require a form or account on its provider’s website. Only a resource explicitly labelled “Free on PianoGrid” offers local materials.

Does playback check my playing?

No. Playback demonstrates the selected version or exercise. There is no microphone or MIDI performance assessment in this module.

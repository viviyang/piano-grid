## Choose a version, not just a title

The same song may have a one-hand melody, an alternating-hands arrangement or a fuller piano part. Difficulty belongs to the version. Publisher level labels are kept separate from PianoGrid’s skill descriptions.

## Know where the learning happens

An external version opens on its provider’s website. A local practice exercise has its own score and sound demonstration here only after those materials are available. Neither route claims to listen to your playing.

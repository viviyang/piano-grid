The E major chord uses E, G-sharp and B. G-sharp is the major third above E; B is the perfect fifth. The displayed root-position example is E4–G♯4–B4. Numbers after note letters identify octaves, not fingers. Use the diagram, note list and bass label together; Play chord sounds the selected notes together, while the note-by-note control sounds them from low to high.

What to notice

The triad has one black key, G-sharp. The key signature of E major contains more sharps, but a chord symbol E does not ask you to play all the notes of that scale. It names this particular three-note chord. E to G-sharp is four semitones; E to B is seven. G-sharp and A-flat can share a piano key, but G-sharp is the third-letter spelling above E. An E major triad is therefore written E–G♯–B, not E–A♭–B.

Chord reference

Common names: E, E major, Emaj.

Intervals from the root: perfect unison, major third, perfect fifth.

Find the notes on your keyboard

  1. Locate E, the white key immediately to the right of a group of two black keys.
  2. Find G-sharp between G and A, then B immediately before the next C.
  3. Use E4–G♯4–B4 for the root example. Check G-sharp carefully before you play the three notes together.

E major and E minor differ at the third

Keep E and B fixed. E major uses G-sharp, while E minor uses G natural. Test the difference with E4–G♯4–B4 and E4–G4–B4.

The root-position builder on this page still asks for E major. A written comparison is useful for self-checking, but it does not add an E-minor mode or claim to assess your playing.

An E chord can lead to A major or A minor

In A major, the chord on scale degree 5 is E major. In an A-minor context, using G-sharp supplies the raised leading tone A minor often uses in its dominant chord. The destination and surrounding harmony supply that context.

Compare E4–G♯4–B4 with E4–A4–C5. The latter is Am/E, not root-position Am, because E is the bass. The shared E stays fixed while the other two notes move.

Questions about E major

Which key is G-sharp?

The black key between G and A. It is the only black key in the root-position E major example.

Does E mean E major or E minor?

A plain E chord symbol normally denotes the major triad. Em specifies E minor.

What does E/G-sharp mean?

An E major chord with G-sharp as the lowest sounding note: its first inversion.

Why is E/B still E major?

Moving B into the bass changes the inversion, not the E–G-sharp–B chord-tone set.

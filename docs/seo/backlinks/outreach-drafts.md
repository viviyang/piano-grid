SLUGLINE: Free browser piano reference for chords, scales, and labeled notes. No account.

THE IDEA:
Looking up piano chords, inversions, and scales while practicing should not require an account or a course. PianoGrid is a free browser reference for piano. You open a page, see the keys on a keyboard diagram, and hear the notes. There is no signup wall. It is built for the lookup job at the instrument: chords, inversions, scales, labeled keyboard notes, and printable blank staff paper. The site does not try to replace lessons or a DAW. It just makes the reference step quiet and immediate.

THE FOUNDER'S STORY:
I built PianoGrid because I needed a quieter piano reference while practicing. Existing tools were useful, but they often mixed ads, logins, or a full lesson path with the simple job of seeing which keys to press. I wanted a small site I could keep open beside the keyboard: chords and inversions, scales, labeled notes, and blank staff paper, with no account. I shipped a public version in 2026 and kept the scope to lookup rather than a course.

WHO IS THE CUSTOMER:
The typical visitor is a beginner or returning pianist who already has an instrument and needs to look up a chord, inversion, scale, or note on the keyboard. Teachers and self-taught adults also use it as a quick visual. People can be anywhere; the site is in English and runs in the browser on a phone or laptop. It is not aimed at DAW producers or people looking for a paid curriculum.

LESSONS TO SHARE:
First, keep the job small. A chord lookup is not a course, and mixing the two made other tools harder to use at the keyboard. Second, do not put a signup wall in front of a reference page. Third, ship the pages people actually open while practicing before adding extra features: chords, inversions, scales, labeled notes, and blank staff paper.

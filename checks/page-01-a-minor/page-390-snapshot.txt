  - region "What makes this chord minor?":
    - heading "What makes this chord minor?" [level=2]
    - paragraph: "From A to C is a minor third: three semitone steps. From C to E is a major third: four semitone steps. Together, the outer notes A and E form a perfect fifth."
    - paragraph: Compare A minor, A–C–E, with A major, A–C♯–E. Only the third changes. A minor uses C natural; A major uses C-sharp. The interval pattern identifies the chord as minor, without needing a mood label.
